Prompt: Sometimes my shadow sings louder than I do

Sometimes my shadow sings louder than I do. My thoughts become clearer when I stop talking. This week, I’ve been learning how to breathe in the silence. How to let the sun take over for a while. When it rains and my head is heavy, I take my shoes off, wade through the puddles by the road, and try to remember what it felt like the last time I danced without shoes on.

In the midst of this quiet time, the Holy Spirit came and whispered, “I want to teach you how to be still.”

And that’s exactly what he did. Every day this week, I’ve sensed him gently urging me away from the noise and chaos. As if he’d wrapped his arms around me and led me aside to a private corner of the room where I could hear his voice clearly and learn to rest.
