Prompt: In the city lights I find myself

In the city lights I find myself

A mirror, a reflection of my own self

Midnight sky with its own soul’s melody

The moon whispers soft secrets to the sea

Echoes from the night

I listen intently

Sometimes I hear you there

In the heartbeat of the street and the rhythm of the air

My ears open up to what has been denied

The sounds of my heart crying for a bye

You call me by name, and I can hear it so loud

That when I close my eyes, it makes my insides shudder

I want to know who I am without your voice

But I can’t seem to make out any other choice.
